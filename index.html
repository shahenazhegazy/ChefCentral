Beyond the catalogue

Great kitchens deserve a closer connection to the source.

Chef Central is inspired by the chefs and hospitality entrepreneurs who care deeply about what they serve. They want exceptional local ingredients, but running a kitchen leaves little time to travel across Egypt in search of them.

Our idea is to do that discovery work for them: seek out growers and makers with distinctive products, taste carefully, and bring the most promising finds together in one professional assortment.

By connecting the craft of Egyptian producers with the ambition of chefs, we want to make local quality easier to source—and more exciting to put on the menu.
